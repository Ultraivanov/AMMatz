## Change Plans

- `M-03-T6`: Replace the long production process list with engineering cards for feedstock, plasma arc, atomization, classification, and AM powder output. Replace the recycling feedstock list with a three-card route: accepted feedstock, plasma processing, and AM-ready powder. Keep hero, navigation, RFQ, and visual system unchanged except for required card rendering support.
- `M-03-T7`: Remove the generic Capability 01/02/03 teaser layer because it reads as marketing material for an engineering audience. Keep the top navigation as the page map and let technical sections prove the claims through content, images, process cards, and parameters. Integrate the Process know-how block inside Production Platform instead of making it a standalone marketing section.
- `M-03-T8`: Rebuild the page hierarchy as claim, credibility, material specification, production route, recycling route, and RFQ. Move Process know-how immediately after the hero and rewrite section labels, H2s, and subheaders so labels provide navigation context while H2s state precise technical ideas.
